Case study 3 · Own product

Trusted Malta

An independent review platform for Malta and Gozo. People search local businesses, read real experiences and leave reviews. Business owners claim their profile and build a reputation from genuine customer feedback.

RoleFounder, product designer, frontend
ScopeBrand, web platform, social
MarketMalta and Gozo
Goal10,000+ businesses listed
Visit website ↗
For people

Search and compare

Find a business by name or service, browse categories like restaurants, hairdressers, gyms and mechanics, and compare ratings.

For businesses

Claim your profile

Owners claim their listing, answer reviews and turn good service into visible proof for the next customer.

Trust

Every review is checked

Open to everyone, no pay-to-play. Reviews are screened to spot fakes, and each listing also shows its Google rating for context.

Design decisions

Built to feel as credible as the big review sites while staying local.

  • IdentityA star cut into a speech-bubble shape in fresh green on near-black. It reads as both "rating" and "voice", and stays legible as a small app icon.
  • HomepageSearch first: one field, one green button, and quick links to write a review or claim a business, over a photo of a real Maltese street.
  • Listings"Best in" rows per category with logo, location, platform score and Google rating side by side, so new listings never look empty.
  • GrowthOrganic social on @trustedmalta with review-led content that speaks to both customers and business owners.
